House rules

Home cooking. Your data stays home.

Kitchen runs every app on your own machine. Your customer list, your contracts and your numbers never pass through our servers. We can't see them, because they never come to us.

  • Runs on your Mac. Not in someone else's cloud.
  • Works offline. Pull the Wi-Fi cable, it keeps going.
  • Nothing uploaded. No telemetry on your data. Ever.

Questions

Things people usually ask.

Do I need to know how to code?

Not at all. If you can install an app on your Mac, you can use Kitchen. The whole idea is that you never have to open a terminal.

Where does my data actually go?

Nowhere. The app runs on your machine and your files stay there. Once an app is set up, it keeps working with the Wi-Fi off.

Which apps can I run?

Anything on our menu, which we test and package ourselves. You can also paste a link to most open-source projects on GitHub and Kitchen will have a go at cooking it.

Can my team use it too?

Yes. Once an app is running, you can share it with your team in one click. They don't need to set anything up.

Is it only for Mac?

For now, yes. We'd rather do one platform properly than three platforms badly.

How much will it cost?

We'll share pricing before launch. Early users and design partners are helping us get it right, so it's a good time to have a say.
